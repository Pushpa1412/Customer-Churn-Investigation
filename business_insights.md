# Customer Churn Business Insights

## 1. Project Overview
This project investigates customer churn patterns using a simulated subscription
dataset containing 1,200 customer records.

The objective is to identify customer segments associated with higher
churn and suggest practical retention strategies.

## 2. Key Findings

### Overall Churn
The overall customer churn rate is **47.58%**.

### Churn by Contract Type

|Contract Type | Customers | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Month-to-month | 660 | 58.18% |
| One year | 302 | 38.08% |
| Two year | 238 | 30.25% |

Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate, while two-year contract 
customers have the lowest.

### Average Customer Tenure

| Customer Group | Average Tenure |
|---|---:|
| Retained | 42.50 months |
| Churned | 30.57 months |

Customers who churned had a shorter average tenure than retained customers.

### Average Support Tickets

| Customer Group | Average Support Tickets |
|---|---:|
| Retained | 1.22 |
| Churned | 1.51 |

Churned customers had a higher average number of support tickets.

### Average Monthly Charges

| Customer Group | Average Monthly Charges |
|---|---:|
| Retained | 74.31 |
| Churned | 76.19 |

Churned customers had slightly higher average monthly charges.

## 3. Business Recommendations

1. Review the customer experience for month-to-month subscribers and test 
suitable incentives for longer-term contracts.
2. Improve onboarding and early engagement to support customers during their 
initial months.
3. Investigate frequent support issues and improve response and resolution 
processes.
4. Review pricing and perceived value, especially for customers with higher 
monthly charges.
5. Monitor churn rates across contract types and customer tenure groups 
regularly.

## 4. Limitations

- The dataset is simulated and does not represent actual company customers.
- The data was generated using predefined assumptions.
- The analysis identifies associations, not proven causes of churn.
- Recommendations should be tested against real, authorized business data 
before implementation.

## 5. Conclusion

The analysis indicates that month-to-month customers, customers with shorter 
tenure, and customers with more support tickets are associated with higher 
churn in this simulated dataset.

These findings can help demonstrate how data analysis supports customer 
retention planning. Further validation using real-world data is recommended.
